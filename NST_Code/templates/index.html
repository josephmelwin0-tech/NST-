The application uses a trained neural network that maps spatial content layout of one image to the visual art style of another. By matching standard feature mean and standard deviation (AdaIN) in real-time, the decoder reconstructs the merged result instantly.

This demo runs completely on your local host environment, processing image transformations on your own CPU or GPU.

The decoder is trained utilizing a combination of COCO for content images and WikiArt for diverse painting and artistic reference styles.
